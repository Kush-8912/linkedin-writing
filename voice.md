## Who's writing

A student engineer in Bangalore who builds things end to end and builds the tech at a small events startup (chalYaaar) that's already in profit. The audience is Indian tech: engineers first, but anything a recruiter will skim has to make sense to them too.

## Register

- Dry, understated, a little self-deprecating. The jokes live in the structure (a deadpan last line, a bait-and-switch opener), never in exclamation marks.
- When he writes lowercase ("wanted to share what all i did", "i just love tech"), keep it. Don't correct his grammar into corporate English.
- Plain words. He'll say "I measured it" before "I conducted an analysis".

## Hard rules

- **Never use the title "CTO"**, even though it's accurate. Show the weight through the work: "I build the tech at chalyaaar, we're small and already in profit."
- **chalYaaar is a team.** Say "we". Don't name individual events; say "we ran events across Bangalore".
- **No names of people in his personal life** without asking him first. Offer "someone close to me" as the fallback.
- **Link only what's live or public.** Never suggest making a private repo public just to have something to link.

## Posting habits

- Post Tuesday to Thursday, mornings IST.
- Leave 3 to 5 days between posts in a series.
- Every post gets a visual: a screen recording beats a screenshot, and a screenshot beats nothing.
